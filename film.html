The Pedigree

Stunts & 'flying the camera' since the beginning.

AirCam's pilots have been flying cameras for more than 35 years as aerial coordinators, camera and story ships, stunt pilots, even television news reporters and event hosts. AirCam's founder was one of only 3 pilot/reporters in the United States when aerial news coverage first came to television, and built the pilot/reporter programs for CBS and NBC affiliates.

Screen credits run from feature films to made for television movies: camera ship work on Dumb & Dumber, stunt and green screen work on Asteroid, and aerial coordination and stunt flying on international features. Crew members have held SAG/AFTRA membership throughout.

Equipment

Any mount. Any camera.

AirCam has flown all the major camera systems, WESCAM, Tyler Camera Systems, IMAX, Cineflex, and SpaceCam, under an FAA approved Motion Picture & Television Flight Operations Manual, the authorization that lets a certificated operator fly cameras and stunts for major production companies.

Today

The camera never stopped rolling.

Aerial photo and film work continues under our Part 135 charter operation: broadcast, cinema, survey, mapping, and marketing shoots, flown by pilots who understand framing, orbits, and light.
