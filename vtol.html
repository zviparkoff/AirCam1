Why KAPA

The proving ground the eVTOL era needs.

Centennial Airport sits 15 miles southeast of downtown Denver at 5,885 feet, with a Class D control tower and 3 runways. It is one of the busiest general aviation airports in the country and the front door to a metro area of 3 million people.

Density altitude is the honest test of any vertical lift aircraft. An aircraft that performs here performs anywhere. That is what makes KAPA more than a market. It is a proving ground.

The infrastructure exists today.

Everything an aircraft program needs on the ground is already running at Centennial Vertiport, the dedicated helicopter and VTOL facility at KAPA.

✓

Certificated Air Carrier

AirCam Vertical operates under FAA Air Carrier Certificate VMIA360T, held since 1991. Operational control, crew training, and dispatch discipline already in place.

✓

Part 145 Maintenance

Certificated airframe, powerplant, and avionics maintenance on the field, backed by Centennial Aero-Tech's shop capability.

✓

Electric MRO with H55

In partnership with H55, the family is establishing the first authorized electric aircraft MRO capability in the United States.

✓

Fuel & Line Service

Jet A and 100LL on site, line service 7 days a week and expanding toward 24 hour operations. Charging infrastructure for electric aircraft is being built.

✓

Hangar & Facilities

Heated hangar, office space, and crew rest areas built around vertical flight operations, with room to base a program.

✓

Pilot Pipeline

Centennial Flyers, one of the largest flying clubs in the country, is bringing H55 powered Bristell B23 Energic electric trainers to Colorado.
